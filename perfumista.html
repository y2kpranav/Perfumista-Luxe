The Anatomy of a Scent

Reading the pyramid.

Every fragrance unfolds in three acts. Knowing them is the fastest way to tell if a scent will actually suit you — before you've even worn it home.

01
Top NotesThe first ten minutes. Citrus, pepper, light fruit — what greets the room.
02
Heart NotesThe true character, from florals to spice, once the top notes fade.
03
Base NotesWoods, musk, amber — what lingers on skin and fabric hours later.
Our Story

From a living-room cabinet to a shop in Noble Park.

It started as a hobby — a personal collection of favourite fragrances kept in an LED glass cabinet, brought out for friends over coffee. What began as free samples and comfortable Moroccan sofas outgrew the living room fast.

Today the same passion fills a small shop in the heart of Noble Park: over a thousand niche and designer fragrances, decanted and sold by someone who still remembers exactly why you loved the first one.

"Every scent gets sampled before it's sold — nothing leaves the counter unexplained."
